\section{Optimizations}
Targeting a 100 MHz frequency on Cyclone IV required structural refactoring. Key optimizations focused on reducing combinational depth and mapping to dedicated resources.

\subsection{Arithmetic Rights Shifts}
Standard division (\texttt{/}) was replaced by bitwise scaling via the \texttt{div1024\_f} function (arithmetic right shifts \texttt{>>> 10}). This decreased synthesis runtime by $>$ 70\% and minimized logic gate utilization.

\subsection{32-Stage Pipelined Divider}
The $Q/I$ ratio for the Quad-Arctan algorithm was moved from a combinational divider to a 32-stage \textbf{Pipelined Restoring Divider}. This maintains a throughput of 1 sample/cycle while isolating the 32-bit division into discrete clock cycles.

\subsection{Structural Pipelining}
Pipelining was applied to three critical modules:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{FIR Filter}: 2-stage structure capturing products before the adder tree.
    \item \textbf{Demodulator}: Reduced logic depth per cycle from 51 levels to 18.
    \item \textbf{De-emphasis}: Registered IIR multipliers (\texttt{prod0}, \texttt{prod1}), halving the recursive path delay.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{DSP Mapping and Unrolling}
Synthesis pragmas were used to map 249 products into dedicated DSP hardware, improving timing slack by $\approx$ 2.5 ns. FIR loops were fully unrolled to execute all taps in parallel within the 2-stage pipeline.

\subsection{Summary of Performance Gains}
Progressive optimizations led to a frequency increase from $<$ 20 MHz to 89.6 MHz (Table \ref{tab:synthesis_history}).
